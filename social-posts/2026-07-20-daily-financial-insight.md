# 💡 Daily Financial Insight — July 20, 2026

**The 50/30/20 rule is a great start. But it's a guess. Your own numbers aren't.**

Most budgeting advice tells you to split your income: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings. It's a fine rule of thumb — but it's the same ratio for everyone, whether you're 24 and renting or 45 and paying off a home loan.

The number that actually matters is *your* savings rate — the % of income you keep, every single month, tracked against your real expenses, investments, and loan payments. Aim for 30%+ consistently and you compound your way to financial independence years faster than any generic split gets you there.
